# Privacy Policy Calcivia OptiProfit

*Last updated: October 2026*

Calcivia OptiProfit ("we", "our", or "us") provides a simple product margin and price calculation tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Calcivia OptiProfit operates primarily as an offline calculation tool. We do not require account registration, nor do we collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

## 2. Local Data Storage
Any calculation inputs or history items created within the application are saved locally on your device for user convenience and can be cleared at any time. This data is never uploaded to external servers.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The application does not integrate third-party data tracking services or advertising SDKs that gather user identities.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, as no personal data is collected from any user.
